## How they were used

- `googlenotes.md` provided the main topic flow and study narrative.
- The large glossary document supplied term coverage and helped normalize definitions.
- The control and compliance documents supplied audit language, control categories, and checklist content.
- The Botium Toys report supplied the case-study facts and remediation priorities.
- The Course 3 network notes inside `googlenotes.md` supplied network device, TCP/IP, protocol, firewall, proxy, VPN, Wi-Fi, and cloud networking coverage.
- The SQL reference supplied query, filter, join, and aggregate examples.
- The Linux file-permissions exemplar supplied the worked `ls -la` and `chmod` scenario.
- The home asset inventory exemplar supplied a simple asset inventory and classification model.
- The risk-register exemplar supplied likelihood, severity, and priority scoring examples.
- The Course 5 glossary supplied asset security, vulnerability management, malware, injection, and threat modeling terms.
- The new Course 5 activity templates supplied portfolio-practice scenarios for asset inventory, risk registers, removable-media risk, data leaks, access control, and PASTA threat modeling.
- The accounting exercise supplied event-log and employee-directory evidence for identifying a stale contractor account with administrative access.
- The PASTA PowerPoints supplied the simplified sneaker app data flow and attack tree concepts that were redrawn as accessible SVG study diagrams.

## Editorial approach

- Repeated glossary content was reduced and paraphrased.
- Raw course formatting noise was removed.
- The result was reorganized into topic-first markdown for easier revision and sharing.
